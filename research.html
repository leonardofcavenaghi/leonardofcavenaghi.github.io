Research Overview & Specialties

I. Theory of Atoms, Quantum Connections & Birational Geometry

The Theory of Atoms (initiated by Katzarkov–Kontsevich–Pantev–Yu) combines Hodge structures with genus-zero Gromov–Witten invariants over a non-Archimedean field $\mathbb{k}$ to decompose the tangent bundle of the $A$-model Frobenius manifold $B_X$ under the Mumford–Tate group $\mathrm{Hod}$ and the Euler operator $\kappa_b = \mathrm{Eu}_b \star_b$ into elementary building blocks—Hodge atoms—that behave predictably under blow-ups.

In joint work with L. Katzarkov and M. Kontsevich (arXiv:2509.15831), we extended this framework to $G$-equivariant birational geometry and initiated the theory of Chen–Ruan atoms for Deligne–Mumford stacks/orbifolds $[X/G]$, unifying Hodge atoms with the modular symbols of Kontsevich–Pestun–Tschinkel and Burnside invariants of Kresch–Tschinkel to produce new examples of non-$G$-linearizable actions on rational varieties and quadric intersections. Recent developments include cyclic generic factors and regularized exponents of the quantum connection (arXiv:2610.06669), root-theoretic localization and atomic decompositions of complete intersections in flag varieties (arXiv:2609.21914), and an atomic proof of Shokurov's conic-bundle conjecture (arXiv:2610.41174).

II. GEOATOMS: Computational Quantum Cohomology & AI

Populating the "periodic table of atoms" across families of projective varieties requires exact genus-zero Gromov–Witten and quantum-connection computations at scale. I lead the development of GEOATOMS (website & catalogue · GitHub), an open computational infrastructure and Python/SageMath suite (gwflags, gwtoric, gwapp).

The library computes genus-0 Gromov–Witten invariants and small quantum multiplication matrices $c_1(TX)\star$ for arbitrary simple or product flag varieties $G/P$, toric varieties, and their complete intersections via Graber–Pandharipande virtual localization, Billey's Schubert restriction formula, center-rooted AHU tree hashing, and two-point exact rational evaluation—supporting a public catalogue of 131+ completed symbolic datasets and AI-assisted pattern discovery.

III. Symplectic Geometry, Mirror Symmetry & Generalized Geometry

My work in mathematical physics and complex/symplectic geometry explores mirror symmetry and dualities beyond the Kähler realm. With L. Grama and L. Katzarkov (Advances in Mathematics, 2026), we constructed a geometric realization of spherical T-duality for oriented $S^3$-bundles over $S^4$ (Milnor bundles) via $\star$-diagrams and higher-dimensional generalized logarithmic transformations, showing that $S^1$-stabilized homotopy 7-spheres (such as $S^7 \times S^1$ and $\Sigma^7_{GM} \times S^1$) are linked by explicit cut-and-paste transformations, and we study mirror symmetry for homotopy Hopf manifolds (with D. Auroux, L. Grama, L. Katzarkov).

In collaboration with L. Grama, L. Katzarkov, P. A. Muniz Martins, M. de Andrade Pinto, and L. C. B. Maia (arXiv:2604.19303, arXiv:2609.00254, arXiv:2610.20153), we study non-Kähler SYZ mirror symmetry for solvmanifolds via the Fourier–Mukai transform, classify nilpotent SYZ mirror pairs, and analyze invariant generalized complex structures and generalized Dolbeault, Bott–Chern, and Aeppli cohomologies on almost abelian solvmanifolds.

IV. Differential Geometry, Curvature, Foliations & Exotic Structures

A foundational pillar of my research concerns how symmetry and metric deformations control curvature, topology, and smooth structures:

  • Cheeger Deformations & $\star$-Diagrams: Introducing $\star$-diagrams (IMRN, 2020) and sharp curvature estimates for Cheeger deformations on $G$-manifolds and fiber bundles (Collect. Math. 2024 — Distinguished Paper Award; Adv. Geom. 2022; São Paulo J. Math. Sci. 2023).
  • Fat Bundles & Symmetric Spaces: Classification and rigidity of fat Riemannian submersions (Internat. J. Math. 2026), resolving the Petersen–Wilhelm conjecture for principal $S^3$/$\mathrm{SO}(3)$ and homogeneous submersions (arXiv:2207.10749), and realizing symmetric spaces as cotangent bundles of flag manifolds to construct counterexamples to the converse of the Soul Conjecture (Rev. Mat. Complut. 2024).
  • Singular Riemannian Foliations (SRFs) & Flows: Extending Palais' Principle of Symmetric Criticality to SRFs to solve $p$-Kirchhoff variational problems (Ann. Mat. Pura Appl. 2026), mean curvature flow on noncompact SRFs (Diff. Geom. Appl. 2020), and homogeneous Ricci flow dynamics on $\mathrm{SU}(3)/T^2$ (Mat. Contemp. 2024).
  • Exotic Smooth Structures & Spacetimes: Proving that basic Laplacian spectra cannot distinguish exotic smooth structures (Adv. Nonlinear Stud. 2025), establishing the $G$-equivariant Kazdan–Warner scalar curvature four-fold classification (arXiv:2106.14709), and constructing geodesically complete Lorentzian metrics with positive Ricci curvature on exotic spheres (Diff. Geom. Appl. 2024).

Publications & Preprints

Preprints & Submitted Manuscripts (14)

  1. [P14] An atomic approach to Shokurov's conic-bundle conjecture (joint with Ludmil Katzarkov, Daniel Pomerleano, and Zhijia Zhang)
    Preprint (October 2026, 25 pp.) arXiv:2610.41174 PDF
    Summary
    Applies a weaker version of Hodge atoms to prove Shokurov's conjecture on the rationality of conic bundle threefolds, establishing that a standard conic bundle over a smooth complex projective rational surface $S$ with discriminant divisor $\Delta$ is irrational whenever $|2K_S + \Delta| \neq \emptyset$. As a consequence, determines precisely which nodal quartic double solids are rational.
  2. [P13] On the generalized cohomologies of almost abelian solvmanifolds (joint with Mauro de Andrade Pinto, Letícia Camponês do Brasil Maia, and Pedro Antonio Muniz Martins)
    Preprint (October 2026) arXiv:2610.20153 PDF
    Summary
    Investigates generalized Dolbeault, Bott–Chern, and Aeppli cohomologies associated with invariant generalized complex structures on almost abelian Lie algebras $\mathfrak{g}_A = \mathbb{R}e_0 \ltimes_A \mathfrak{h}$ and solvmanifolds $\Gamma \backslash G_A$. Identifies invariant generalized Dolbeault cohomology with the Chevalley–Eilenberg cohomology of an almost abelian complex Lie algebra $\mathcal{L}$, yielding explicit combinatorial dimension formulas and injection theorems into compact quotients.
  3. [P12] A quantum connection approach to rationality (joint with Luiz Carazolli, Ludmil Katzarkov, Maxim Kontsevich, and Pedro Antonio Muniz Martins)
    Preprint (October 2026) arXiv:2610.06669 PDF
    Summary
    Extracts ranks and regularized exponents from cyclic generic factors of the quantum connection and proves their persistence under birational transformations. Applies these quantum-connection invariants to establish non-rationality for cubic threefolds, ordinary Gushel–Mukai threefolds, very general quartic fourfolds, and products $X \times \mathbb{P}^1$ of those threefolds with the projective line.
  4. [P11] On the atomic decomposition of complete intersection in flag varieties (joint with Boris Alexeev, Giovane Galindo, Bogdan Georgiev, Ludmil Katzarkov, and Pedro Antonio Muniz Martins)
    Preprint (September 2026; revised October 2026) arXiv:2609.21914 PDF GEOATOMS Code & Catalogue
    Summary
    Develops a root-theoretic torus localization formalism expressing genus-zero Gromov–Witten invariants of flag varieties and smooth complete intersections as sums over decorated trees, connecting them to the KKPY Theory of Hodge Atoms via quantum multiplication by $c_1(X)$. Establishes spectral criteria on the monodromy-fixed block for irrationality and K3-surface blowup centers of Fano fourfolds with $h^{3,1}(X)=1$, proving the irrationality of very general complete intersections in $\operatorname{LG}(3,6)$, $G_2/P_2$, and the spinor $10$-fold.
  5. [P10] On the generalized geometry of almost abelian solvmanifolds (joint with Mauro de Andrade Pinto, Letícia Camponês do Brasil Maia, and Pedro Antonio Muniz Martins)
    Preprint (August 2026; revised September 2026) arXiv:2609.00254 PDF
    Summary
    Studies left-invariant generalized complex structures on almost abelian Lie groups $G_A$ and their compact quotients $\Gamma \backslash G_A$. Characterizes all admissible types and closed invariant pure-spinor generators via eigenvalue pairings when $A$ is $\mathbb{R}$-diagonalizable, establishes Jordan-theoretic type bounds for general $A$, and provides explicit intermediate-type constructions and nonexistence theorems in dimension six.
  6. [P9] The Cohomology of Solvmanifold SYZ Mirrors (joint with Lino Grama, Ludmil Katzarkov, and Pedro Antonio Muniz Martins)
    Preprint (April 2026) arXiv:2604.19303 PDF
    Summary
    Investigates non-Kähler Strominger–Yau–Zaslow (SYZ) mirror symmetry for dual torus fibrations over solvmanifolds in the Lau–Tseng–Yau framework, proving that the Fourier–Mukai transform exchanges type-A special Lagrangian sections with flat $\mathrm{U}(1)$-connections and type-B line bundles satisfying the deformed Hermitian–Yang–Mills (dHYM) equation. Provides pure Lie-theoretic criteria and a complete classification of nilpotent SYZ mirror pairs, and introduces Tseng–Yau and Bott–Chern mirror bicomplexes whose basic cohomologies are isomorphic under the Fourier–Mukai transform.
  7. [P8] Atoms meet symbols (joint with Ludmil Katzarkov and Maxim Kontsevich)
    Preprint (September 2025; v4 revised April 2026) arXiv:2509.15831 PDF
    Summary
    Unifies the Katzarkov–Kontsevich–Pantev–Yu (KKPY) theory of Hodge atoms with the Kontsevich–Pestun–Tschinkel theory of modular symbols and Burnside groups to create a framework for $G$-equivariant birational geometry, initiating the theory of Chen–Ruan atoms for global quotient orbifolds $[X/G]$. Develops new geometric invariants for $\mathbb{Z}/2$- and $\mathbb{Z}/3$-actions on surfaces and threefolds and produces new examples of non-$G$-linearizable actions on rational and complete intersection varieties.
  8. [P7] A Gromov–Witten approach to $G$-equivariant birational invariants (joint with Lino Grama and Ludmil Katzarkov)
    Preprint (May 2024; v4 revised October 2025) arXiv:2405.07322 PDF
    Summary
    Connects orbifold Chen–Ruan cohomology and Deligne cohomology constructions of Lupercio–Uribe with $G$-equivariant birational invariants (Burnside groups and modular symbols of Kontsevich, Kresch, Pestun, and Tschinkel). Lays the initial programmatic bridge linking equivariant Gromov–Witten invariants, the theory of atoms, and smooth invariants of hypersurface singularity links.
  9. [P6] The complete dynamics description of positively curved metrics in the Wallach flag manifold $\mathrm{SU}(3)/\mathrm{T}^2$ (joint with Lino Grama, Ricardo M. Martins, and Douglas D. Novaes)
    Preprint (July 2023, 15 pp., 12 figures) arXiv:2307.06418 PDF
    Summary
    Provides a complete phase-plane dynamical analysis of the three-parameter family of invariant metrics on the Wallach flag manifold $\mathrm{SU}(3)/\mathrm{T}^2$ restricted to the normalized tetrahedron $x+y+z=1$ under the projected homogeneous Ricci flow. Explicitly delineates all regions of positive sectional, Ricci, intermediate Ricci, and scalar curvature, characterizing curvature sign preservation and escape along the flow as well as induced evolutions on fiber bundles over $\mathrm{SU}(3)/\mathrm{T}^2$.
  10. [P5] Fatness and positive curvatures on Riemannian submersions (joint with Lino Grama and Llohann D. Sperança; formerly titled The Petersen–Wilhelm conjecture on principal bundles)
    Preprint (July 2022; v5 revised August 2026) arXiv:2207.10749 PDF
    Summary
    Introduces new intrinsic notions of fatness for Riemannian submersions—including integral fatness, sub-fatness, weakly nonnegative curvature (WNN), and $A$-Clifford submersions—via dual holonomy fields. Proves that positive sectional curvature implies fatness within the WNN class, thereby verifying the Petersen–Wilhelm fiber dimension conjecture for homogeneous submersions and principal $\mathrm{S}^3$- and $\mathrm{SO}(3)$-bundles, and characterizes $3$-Sasakian geometry in rank three.
  11. [P4] A note on the vertizontal curvature of fat bundles (sole author)
    Preprint (July 2022; v3 revised January 2024) arXiv:2207.10757 PDF
    Summary
    Addresses a question posed by W. Ziller on whether the base metric of a fat principal $G$-bundle ($\dim G = 3$) with totally geodesic orbits can be modified to render all vertical curvatures constant and equal to $1$. Establishes a rigidity theorem for fat Riemannian foliations with bounded holonomy under a point-wise vertizontal curvature condition satisfied by locally symmetric spaces.
  12. [P3] Singular Riemannian Foliations and the prescribing scalar curvature problem (joint with Marcos M. Alexandrino)
    Preprint (November 2021) arXiv:2111.13257 PDF
    Summary
    Proves a Kondrachov embedding theorem and a Palais-type Principle of Symmetric Criticality for basic functions of orbit-like singular Riemannian foliations (including proper Lie groupoid orbits). Applies these analytical tools to study the foliated Yamabe problem and the existence of constant scalar curvature metrics on fiber bundles with homogeneous fibers and on exotic spheres.
  13. [P2] The $G$-equivariant Kazdan–Warner problem (joint with João Marcos do Ó and Llohann D. Sperança; formerly titled The symmetric Kazdan–Warner problem and applications)
    Preprint (June 2021; v5 revised June 2026) arXiv:2106.14709 PDF
    Summary
    Establishes the $G$-equivariant analogue of the Kazdan–Warner scalar curvature trichotomy for closed manifolds ($n \ge 3$) with an effective isometric action of a compact connected Lie group $G$ of cohomogeneity $\ge 1$ and no fixed points. Discovers a novel fourth class—totally $G$-positive pairs $(M,G)$—for which every $G$-invariant metric has positive total scalar curvature and which admit $G$-invariant positive constant scalar curvature metrics but no zero or negative constant scalar curvature metrics.
  14. [P1] A direct approach to prescribing scalar curvature on bundles (joint with Llohann D. Sperança; formerly titled On prescribing scalar curvature on bundles and applications)
    Preprint (December 2020; v4 revised May 2023) arXiv:2012.13272 PDF
    Summary
    Characterizes which smooth functions can be realized as scalar curvature functions on total spaces of fiber bundles—including bundles over exotic manifolds and Calabi–Yau manifolds—by combining Kazdan–Warner perturbation techniques with variational methods for Riemannian submersions.

Published & Accepted Articles (in reverse chronological order) (13)

  1. [J12] A geometric realization of spherical T-duality via $\star$-diagrams (joint with Lino Grama and Ludmil Katzarkov)
    Advances in Mathematics 503 (2026), Article 111202. Published Version (DOI) arXiv:2404.19088
    Summary
    Relates spherical T-duality for oriented linear $\mathrm{S}^3$-bundles over $\mathrm{S}^4$ (Milnor bundles) to $\star$-diagrams and higher-dimensional generalized logarithmic transformations. Proves that after $\mathrm{S}^1$-stabilization, spherical T-dualities between homotopy $7$-spheres—including the passage from $\mathrm{S}^7 \times \mathrm{S}^1$ to $\Sigma^7_{GM} \times \mathrm{S}^1$ for the Gromoll–Meyer exotic sphere—are realized by explicit cut-and-paste generalized logarithmic transformations.
  2. [J11] Singular Riemannian foliations, variational problems and principles of symmetric criticalities (joint with Marcos M. Alexandrino, Diego Corro, and Marcelo K. Inagaki)
    Annali di Matematica Pura ed Applicata 205, no. 5 (2026), 2507–2572. Published Version (DOI) arXiv:2311.07058
    Summary
    Establishes an analogue of Palais' Principle of Symmetric Criticality for $\mathcal{F}$-symmetric integral operators on Banach Sobolev spaces $W^{1,p}(M)$ over compact manifolds equipped with singular Riemannian foliations (including isoparametric foliations). Combined with a foliated Rellich–Kondrachov–Hebey–Vaugon embedding theorem, this bypasses classical Sobolev critical exponent obstructions and yields infinitely many weak solutions to $p$-Kirchhoff-type variational problems.
  3. [J10] A curvature approach to fatness (joint with Lino Grama)
    International Journal of Mathematics 37, nos. 5–6 (2026), Article 2542001. Published Version (DOI) arXiv:2212.08154
    Summary
    Investigates the structural geometry of fat Riemannian submersions with non-negative sectional curvature. Classifies their fibers as symmetric spaces, establishes an isometric correspondence between fat foliations and coset foliations on Lie groups, and proves rigidity theorems for dual foliations associated with fat submersions.
  4. [A1] The curvature of convex sum of metrics and applications (joint with Giovane Galindo and Llohann D. Sperança)
    Accepted for publication in Differential Geometry in Latin America 2025. arXiv:2106.14781 PDF
    Summary
    Derives explicit formulas for the Riemann curvature tensor along a convex combination of Riemannian metrics $g_t = (1-t)g_0 + t g_1$ and establishes necessary and sufficient higher-order conditions ($r \ge 2$) for increasing the average sectional curvature along an immersed totally geodesic flat torus, with applications to conformal deformations, vertical warpings, and Cheeger deformations.
  5. [J9] Hearing exotic smooth structures (joint with João Marcos do Ó and Llohann D. Sperança)
    Advanced Nonlinear Studies 25, no. 4 (2025), 1112–1126. Published Version (DOI) arXiv:2402.10106
    Summary
    Studies basic eigenvalues and eigenfunctions of the Riemannian Laplacian on closed manifolds with effective isometric compact Lie group actions. Demonstrates that homeomorphic but non-diffeomorphic manifolds (such as exotic homotopy spheres) can carry invariant metrics with identical basic Laplacian spectra and identical rings of invariant admissible scalar curvature functions, proving that basic spectral data cannot distinguish exotic smooth structures.
  6. [J8] Gromoll–Meyer's actions and the geometry of (exotic) spacetimes (joint with Lino Grama)
    Differential Geometry and its Applications 94 (2024), Article 102121. Published Version (DOI) arXiv:2403.08960
    Summary
    Develops an equivariant computational framework (via negative-parameter Cheeger deformations and $\star$-constructions) to build Lorentzian spacetime metrics on classical and exotic spheres, including the Gromoll–Meyer exotic $7$-sphere. Constructs time-oriented, geodesically complete Lorentzian metrics with positive Ricci curvature on homeomorphic but non-diffeomorphic manifolds sharing identical physical properties.
  7. [J7] Symmetric spaces as adjoint orbits and their geometries (joint with Carolina Garcia, Lino Grama, and Luiz A. B. San Martin)
    Revista Matemática Complutense 37, no. 3 (2024), 921–966. Published Version (DOI) arXiv:2302.09638
    Summary
    Realizes classical symmetric spaces (including Berger's semi-Kähler symmetric spaces) as cotangent bundles of symmetric flag manifolds, enabling an explicit description of their geodesics and Lagrangian submanifolds. Uses these bundle constructions to answer a question of W. Ziller in the negative by providing the first examples of vector bundles over simply connected non-negatively curved manifolds that admit no metric of non-negative sectional curvature even though their unit sphere bundles do.
  8. [J6] On the dynamics of positively curved metrics on $\mathrm{SU}(3)/\mathrm{T}^2$ under the homogeneous Ricci flow (joint with Lino Grama and Ricardo M. Martins)
    Matemática Contemporânea 60 (2024), 3–30. Published Version (DOI) arXiv:2305.06119
    Summary
    Shows that the classical Wallach flag manifold $\mathrm{SU}(3)/\mathrm{T}^2$ and generalized flag manifolds $\mathrm{SU}(m+2p)/\mathrm{S}(\mathrm{U}(m)\times\mathrm{U}(p)\times\mathrm{U}(p))$ admit invariant metrics of positive intermediate Ricci curvature ($\mathrm{Ric}_d > 0$ for $d \in \{1,2,3,4,5\}$) that lose positivity along the homogeneous Ricci flow for $d \in \{1,2,3,5\}$.
  9. [J5] Positive Ricci curvature through Cheeger deformations (joint with Renato J. M. e Silva and Llohann D. Sperança)

  10. [J4] The concept of Cheeger deformations on fiber bundles with compact structure group (joint with Lino Grama and Llohann D. Sperança)

  11. [J3] Positive Ricci curvature on fiber bundles with compact structure group (joint with Llohann D. Sperança)

  12. [J2] On mean curvature flow of singular Riemannian foliations: Noncompact cases (joint with Marcos M. Alexandrino and Ícaro Gonçalves)

  13. [J1] On the geometry of some equivariantly related manifolds (joint with Llohann D. Sperança)
