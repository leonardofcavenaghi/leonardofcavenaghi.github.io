Research Overview & Specialties

I. Theory of Atoms, Quantum Connections & Birational Geometry

The Theory of Atoms (introduced by Katzarkov–Kontsevich–Pantev–Yu) combines Hodge structures with genus-zero Gromov–Witten invariants over a non-Archimedean field $\mathbb{k}$. At a general point $b \in B_X^{\mathrm{Hod}}$ in the fixed locus of the group $\mathrm{Hod}$ (acting on $\mathbb{Z}/2$-folded polarizable pure Hodge structures) on the rigid $\mathbb{k}$-analytic Frobenius manifold $B_X$, the tangent algebra $(H \otimes_{\mathbb{Q}} \mathbb{k}, \star_b)$ splits into generalized eigenspaces of the Euler operator $\kappa_b = \mathrm{Eu}_b \star_b$ refined by $\mathrm{Hod}$-representations—its Hodge atoms—which transform predictably under blow-ups.

With L. Katzarkov and M. Kontsevich (arXiv:2509.15831), we unified Hodge atoms with the modular symbols of Kontsevich–Pestun–Tschinkel to construct invariants in $G$-equivariant birational geometry, initiating Chen–Ruan atoms for global quotient orbifolds $[X/G]$ (assuming the blow-up formula for quantum Chen–Ruan cohomology) alongside purely geometric invariants for $\mathbb{Z}/2$- and $\mathbb{Z}/3$-actions on surfaces and threefolds. Subsequent work investigates cyclic generic factors and regularized exponents of the quantum connection (arXiv:2610.06669), root-theoretic localization and spectral criteria for complete intersections in flag varieties (arXiv:2609.21914), and a proof of Shokurov's conic-bundle conjecture via a weaker version of Hodge atoms (arXiv:2610.41174).

II. GEOATOMS: Computational Quantum Cohomology

I contribute to the development of GEOATOMS (website & catalogue · GitHub), an open computational suite and symbolic catalogue in Python/SageMath (gwflags, gwtoric, gwapp) supporting the Theory of Atoms program.

The software computes genus-zero Gromov–Witten invariants and small quantum multiplication operators $c_1(TX)\star$ for generalized flag varieties $G/P$, toric varieties, and supported complete intersections via Graber–Pandharipande virtual localization, Billey's formula, center-rooted AHU tree hashing, and exact two-point rational evaluation. For complete intersections, the returned ambient operator acts on the flag-ambient sector, and the catalogue documents the precise hypotheses required to extract full atomic decompositions across 131+ computed examples.

III. Symplectic Geometry, Mirror Symmetry & Mathematical Physics

With L. Grama and L. Katzarkov (Advances in Mathematics, 2026), we linked spherical T-duality for oriented linear $\mathrm{S}^3$-bundles over $\mathrm{S}^4$ (Milnor bundles) to bifree and pullback $\star$-diagrams and product-preserving generalized logarithmic transformations on $\Sigma \times \mathrm{S}^1$, showing that after $\mathrm{S}^1$-stabilization spherical T-dualities between homotopy $7$-spheres—including the passage from $\mathrm{S}^7 \times \mathrm{S}^1$ to $\Sigma^7_{GM} \times \mathrm{S}^1$—are realized by explicit cut-and-paste operations.

With L. Grama, L. Katzarkov, P. A. Muniz Martins, M. de Andrade Pinto, and L. C. B. Maia (arXiv:2604.19303, arXiv:2609.00254, arXiv:2610.20153), we study non-Kähler SYZ mirror symmetry for solvmanifolds in the Lau–Tseng–Yau framework, classify nilpotent SYZ mirror pairs, and analyze invariant generalized complex structures and generalized Dolbeault, Bott–Chern, and Aeppli cohomologies on almost abelian solvmanifolds.

IV. Differential Geometry, Curvature, Foliations & Exotic Structures

A major part of my work studies how isometric symmetries, submersions, and metric deformations interact with curvature and smooth topology:

  • Cheeger Deformations & $\star$-Diagrams: Introducing $\star$-diagrams (IMRN, 2020) and sharp curvature estimates for Cheeger deformations on $G$-manifolds and fiber bundles (Collect. Math. 2024 — Distinguished Paper Award; Adv. Geom. 2022; São Paulo J. Math. Sci. 2023).
  • Fat Submersions & Symmetric Spaces: Structural classification and dual-foliation rigidity of fat Riemannian submersions (Internat. J. Math. 2026); intrinsic fatness, weakly nonnegative curvature (WNN) for positively curved submersions, and $A$-Clifford / $3$-Sasakian rigidity on $\mathrm{S}^3$- and $\mathrm{SO}(3)$-principal bundles (arXiv:2207.10749); and realizing classical symmetric spaces as cotangent bundles of symmetric flag manifolds to construct vector bundles over simply connected non-negatively curved manifolds that admit no metric of nonnegative sectional curvature even though their unit sphere bundles do (Rev. Mat. Complut. 2024).
  • Singular Riemannian Foliations (SRFs) & Flows: Extending Palais' Principle of Symmetric Criticality to AVP singular Riemannian foliations for $p$-Kirchhoff variational problems (Ann. Mat. Pura Appl. 2026), mean curvature flow on noncompact generalized isoparametric foliations (Diff. Geom. Appl. 2020), and homogeneous Ricci flow dynamics on $\mathrm{SU}(3)/\mathrm{T}^2$ (Mat. Contemp. 2024).
  • Exotic Smooth Structures & Spacetimes: Showing that basic Laplacian spectra and invariant admissible scalar curvature rings fail to distinguish exotic smooth structures (Adv. Nonlinear Stud. 2025), establishing the four-class $G$-equivariant Kazdan–Warner classification (arXiv:2106.14709), and constructing complete Lorentzian metrics with positive Ricci curvature on exotic spheres (Diff. Geom. Appl. 2024).

Publications & Preprints

Preprints & Submitted Manuscripts (14)

  1. [P14] An atomic approach to Shokurov's conic-bundle conjecture (joint with Ludmil Katzarkov, Daniel Pomerleano, and Zhijia Zhang)
    Preprint (October 2026, 25 pp.) arXiv:2610.41174 PDF
    Summary
    Applies a weaker version of Hodge atoms to prove Shokurov's conjecture on the rationality of conic bundle threefolds, establishing that a standard conic bundle over a smooth complex projective rational surface $S$ with discriminant divisor $\Delta$ is irrational whenever $|2K_S + \Delta| \neq \emptyset$. As a consequence, determines precisely which nodal quartic double solids are rational.
  2. [P13] On the generalized cohomologies of almost abelian solvmanifolds (joint with Mauro de Andrade Pinto, Letícia Camponês do Brasil Maia, and Pedro Antonio Muniz Martins)
    Preprint (October 2026) arXiv:2610.20153 PDF
    Summary
    Studies generalized Dolbeault, Bott–Chern, and Aeppli cohomologies for invariant generalized complex structures on almost abelian Lie algebras $\mathfrak{g}_A = \mathbb{R}e_0 \ltimes_A \mathfrak{h}$ and solvmanifolds $\Gamma \backslash G_A$. Identifies invariant generalized Dolbeault cohomology with the Chevalley–Eilenberg cohomology of the almost abelian complex Lie algebra $\mathcal{L}$ (the $+i$-eigenspace) twisted by the canonical-line character, yielding explicit combinatorial dimension formulas when $A$ is $\mathbb{R}$-diagonalizable and showing that the cohomology of invariant forms injects into that of $\Gamma \backslash G_A$.
  3. [P12] A quantum connection approach to rationality (joint with Luiz Carazolli, Ludmil Katzarkov, Maxim Kontsevich, and Pedro Antonio Muniz Martins)
    Preprint (October 2026) arXiv:2610.06669 PDF
    Summary
    Extracts rank and regularized exponents from cyclic generic factors of the quantum connection and proves that these data persist under the birational comparisons considered. Applications establish non-rationality for cubic threefolds, ordinary Gushel–Mukai threefolds, very general quartic fourfolds, and products $X \times \mathbb{P}^1$ of those threefolds with the projective line.
  4. [P11] On the atomic decomposition of complete intersection in flag varieties (joint with Boris Alexeev, Giovane Galindo, Bogdan Georgiev, Ludmil Katzarkov, and Pedro Antonio Muniz Martins)
    Preprint (September 2026; revised October 2026) arXiv:2609.21914 PDF GEOATOMS Code & Catalogue
    Summary
    Develops a root-theoretic localization formalism for genus-zero Gromov–Witten invariants of flag varieties and smooth zero loci of globally generated homogeneous vector bundles as finite sums over decorated trees, linking them to Hodge atoms via small quantum multiplication by $c_1(X)$. For a Fano fourfold $X$ with $h^{3,1}(X)=1$ arising as a hyperplane section of a suitable Fano fivefold, proves spectral criteria on the monodromy-fixed block (eigenvalue multiplicity $\le 2$ implies irrationality for Hodge-general $X$; Jordan defect $\le 1$ and rationality imply a smooth surface blow-up center birational to a projective K3 surface), and establishes irrationality for very general complete intersections of a hyperplane and a quadric in $\operatorname{LG}(3,6)$, a smooth quadric section in $G_2/P_2$, and five hyperplanes and a quadric in the spinor $10$-fold.
  5. [P10] On the generalized geometry of almost abelian solvmanifolds (joint with Mauro de Andrade Pinto, Letícia Camponês do Brasil Maia, and Pedro Antonio Muniz Martins)
    Preprint (August 2026; revised September 2026) arXiv:2609.00254 PDF
    Summary
    Studies left-invariant generalized complex structures on almost abelian Lie groups $G_A$ and their compact quotients. Characterizes all admissible types and closed invariant pure-spinor generators via eigenvalue pairings when $A$ is $\mathbb{R}$-diagonalizable, obtains Jordan-theoretic type bounds and a construction via a complex quotient and symplectic ideal for general $A$, and establishes six-dimensional nonexistence theorems and explicit intermediate-type examples.
  6. [P9] The Cohomology of Solvmanifold SYZ Mirrors (joint with Lino Grama, Ludmil Katzarkov, and Pedro Antonio Muniz Martins)
    Preprint (April 2026) arXiv:2604.19303 PDF
    Summary
    Investigates non-Kähler SYZ mirror symmetry for dual torus fibrations over solvmanifolds in the Lau–Tseng–Yau framework, proving that the Fourier–Mukai transform exchanges type-A supersymmetric cycles (special Lagrangian sections with flat $\mathrm{U}(1)$-connections) with type-B cycles (line bundles satisfying the deformed Hermitian–Yang–Mills equation). Gives Lie-theoretic criteria yielding new mirror families from almost abelian and generalized Heisenberg Lie groups, completes the classification for nilpotent Lie groups, and introduces Tseng–Yau and Bott–Chern mirror bicomplexes whose enclosed primitive cohomologies are isomorphic on basic forms under the Fourier–Mukai transform.
  7. [P8] Atoms meet symbols (joint with Ludmil Katzarkov and Maxim Kontsevich)
    Preprint (September 2025; v4 revised April 2026) arXiv:2509.15831 PDF
    Summary
    Unifies the Katzarkov–Kontsevich–Pantev–Yu theory of Hodge atoms with the Kontsevich–Pestun–Tschinkel theory of modular symbols to construct invariants in $G$-equivariant birational geometry. Initiates the theory of Chen–Ruan atoms and, assuming the blow-up formula for quantum Chen–Ruan cohomology, outlines how to extend atom theory to global quotient orbifolds $[X/G]$. Independently develops a purely geometric class of invariants for $\mathbb{Z}/2$- and $\mathbb{Z}/3$-actions on surfaces and threefolds, producing new examples of non-$G$-linearizable actions on projective varieties.
  8. [P7] A Gromov–Witten approach to $G$-equivariant birational invariants (joint with Lino Grama and Ludmil Katzarkov)
    Preprint (May 2024; v4 revised October 2025) arXiv:2405.07322 PDF
    Summary
    Programmatic manuscript connecting Chen–Ruan cohomology and orbifold constructions of Lupercio–Uribe with the $G$-equivariant birational invariants of Kontsevich, Kresch, Pestun, and Tschinkel, outlining how combining these tools with the theory of atoms leads to a framework for equivariant atoms and links with smooth invariants such as Kervaire–Milnor invariants.
  9. [P6] The complete dynamics description of positively curved metrics in the Wallach flag manifold $\mathrm{SU}(3)/\mathrm{T}^2$ (joint with Lino Grama, Ricardo M. Martins, and Douglas D. Novaes)
    Preprint (July 2023, 15 pp., 12 figures) arXiv:2307.06418 PDF
    Summary
    Describes all curvature-positivity regions (from positive sectional and intermediate Ricci curvature to positive scalar curvature) inside the normalized simplex $x+y+z=1$ of invariant metrics on the Wallach flag manifold $\mathrm{SU}(3)/\mathrm{T}^2$, analyzing curvature sign preservation and escape under the projected homogeneous Ricci flow as well as induced evolutions on fiber bundles over $\mathrm{SU}(3)/\mathrm{T}^2$.
  10. [P5] Fatness and positive curvatures on Riemannian submersions (joint with Lino Grama and Llohann D. Sperança)
    Preprint (July 2022; v5 major revision August 2026) arXiv:2207.10749 PDF
    Summary
    Guided by properties of dual holonomy fields, introduces intrinsic notions of fatness for Riemannian submersions: integral fatness (implied by positive sectional curvature), sub-fatness (arising in canonical variation limits), and weakly nonnegative curvature (WNN), which depends only on the horizontal distribution $\mathcal{H}$ and base metric $b$. Proves that positive sectional curvature implies fatness within the WNN class (covering homogeneous submersions and beyond), and introduces $A$-Clifford submersions, showing they are intrinsic to $(\mathcal{H},b)$, single out a canonical vertical gauge, and in rank three characterize $3$-Sasakian geometry with new rigidity results on $\mathrm{S}^3$- and $\mathrm{SO}(3)$-principal bundles.
  11. [P4] A note on the vertizontal curvature of fat bundles (sole author)
    Preprint (July 2022; v3 revised January 2024) arXiv:2207.10757 PDF
    Summary
    Addresses a question of W. Ziller on modifying the base metric of a fat principal $G$-bundle ($\dim G = 3$) with totally geodesic orbits to make all vertical curvatures equal to $1$. Establishes a rigidity theorem for fat Riemannian foliations with bounded holonomy and connected compact total spaces under a curvature constraint satisfied on locally symmetric spaces, assuming all vertizontal curvatures coincide at a point.
  12. [P3] Singular Riemannian Foliations and the prescribing scalar curvature problem (joint with Marcos M. Alexandrino)
    Preprint (November 2021) arXiv:2111.13257 PDF
    Summary
    Proves a Kondrachov embedding theorem and a Palais-type Principle of Symmetric Criticality for basic functions of orbit-like singular Riemannian foliations (including proper Lie groupoid orbits). Applies these analytical tools to study the foliated Yamabe problem and constant scalar curvature metrics on fiber bundles with homogeneous fibers and on exotic spheres.
  13. [P2] The $G$-equivariant Kazdan–Warner problem (joint with João Marcos do Ó and Llohann D. Sperança)
    Preprint (June 2021; v5 revised June 2026) arXiv:2106.14709 PDF
    Summary
    Establishes the $G$-equivariant analogue of the Kazdan–Warner scalar curvature trichotomy for closed connected manifolds ($n \ge 3$) with an effective isometric action of a compact connected Lie group $G$ of cohomogeneity at least one and no zero-dimensional orbits. Shows that a distinct fourth class emerges—totally $G$-positive pairs $(M,G)$—for which every $G$-invariant metric has positive total scalar curvature and which admit $G$-invariant positive constant scalar curvature metrics but no zero or negative constant scalar curvature metrics.
  14. [P1] A direct approach to prescribing scalar curvature on bundles (joint with Llohann D. Sperança)
    Preprint (December 2020; v4 revised May 2023) arXiv:2012.13272 PDF
    Summary
    Determines admissible scalar curvature functions on total spaces of fiber bundles—including bundles over exotic manifolds and Calabi–Yau manifolds—by directly applying Kazdan–Warner results together with variational methods for Riemannian submersion metrics.

Published & Accepted Articles (in reverse chronological order) (13)

  1. [J12] A geometric realization of spherical T-duality via $\star$-diagrams (joint with Lino Grama and Ludmil Katzarkov)
    Advances in Mathematics 503 (2026), Article 111202. Published Version (DOI) arXiv:2404.19088
    Summary
    Relates spherical T-duality for oriented linear $\mathrm{S}^3$-bundles over $\mathrm{S}^4$ (Milnor bundles $M_{m,n}$) to $\star$-diagrams and higher-dimensional generalized logarithmic transformations. Characterizes T-duality correspondence spaces of principal pairs as bifree $\star$-diagrams, realizes bundles of equal Euler class $ku$ as base manifolds of an explicit non-bifree pullback $\star$-diagram, and proves that after $\mathrm{S}^1$-stabilization spherical T-dualities between homotopy $7$-spheres—including passing from $\mathrm{S}^7 \times \mathrm{S}^1$ to $\Sigma^7_{GM} \times \mathrm{S}^1$—are implemented by product-preserving generalized logarithmic transformations.
  2. [J11] Singular Riemannian foliations, variational problems and principles of symmetric criticalities (joint with Marcos M. Alexandrino, Diego Corro, and Marcelo K. Inagaki)
    Annali di Matematica Pura ed Applicata 205, no. 5 (2026), 2507–2572. Published Version (DOI) arXiv:2311.07058
    Summary
    Investigates variational problems on compact Riemannian manifolds equipped with AVP singular Riemannian foliations (including isoparametric foliations, SRFs on Euclidean fiber bundles, and isometric Lie group orbits). Proves an analogue of Palais' Principle of Symmetric Criticality for $\mathcal{F}$-symmetric integral operators on $W^{1,p}(M)$ together with an $\mathcal{F}$-basic Rellich–Kondrachov–Hebey–Vaugon embedding theorem, circumventing Sobolev critical exponent obstructions to yield infinitely many weak solutions to $p$-Kirchhoff-type variational problems.
  3. [J10] A curvature approach to fatness (joint with Lino Grama)
    International Journal of Mathematics 37, nos. 5–6 (2026), Article 2542001. Published Version (DOI) arXiv:2212.08154
    Summary
    Explores the structural implications of fat Riemannian submersions, focusing on those with non-negative sectional curvature. Classifies their fibers as symmetric spaces, establishes an isometric correspondence between fat foliations and coset foliations on Lie groups, and proves rigidity theorems for dual foliations associated with fat submersions.
  4. [A1] The curvature of convex sum of metrics and applications (joint with Giovane Galindo and Llohann D. Sperança)
    Accepted for publication in Differential Geometry in Latin America 2025. arXiv:2106.14781 PDF
    Summary

  5. [J9] Hearing exotic smooth structures (joint with João Marcos do Ó and Llohann D. Sperança)
    Advanced Nonlinear Studies 25, no. 4 (2025), 1112–1126. Published Version (DOI) arXiv:2402.10106
    Summary
    Studies basic eigenvalues and eigenfunctions of the Riemannian Laplacian on closed connected manifolds with effective isometric compact Lie group actions. Establishes that specific homeomorphic but non-diffeomorphic homotopy spheres and connected sums carry invariant metrics with identical basic spectra and identical rings of invariant admissible scalar curvature functions.
  6. [J8] Gromoll–Meyer's actions and the geometry of (exotic) spacetimes (joint with Lino Grama)
    Differential Geometry and its Applications 94 (2024), Article 102121. Published Version (DOI) arXiv:2403.08960
    Summary
    Introduces an equivariant computational construction of Lorentzian metrics on classical and exotic spheres (such as the Gromoll–Meyer exotic $7$-sphere), producing geodesically complete, time-oriented Lorentzian metrics with positive Ricci curvature on homeomorphic but non-diffeomorphic manifolds sharing the same geometric and physical properties.
  7. [J7] Symmetric spaces as adjoint orbits and their geometries (joint with Carolina Garcia, Lino Grama, and Luiz A. B. San Martin)
    Revista Matemática Complutense 37, no. 3 (2024), 921–966. Published Version (DOI) arXiv:2302.09638
    Summary
    Realizes classical symmetric spaces (including Berger's semi-Kähler symmetric spaces) as cotangent bundles of symmetric flag manifolds, yielding explicit descriptions of their geodesics and Lagrangian submanifolds. As an application, presents the first examples of vector bundles over simply connected manifolds with nonnegative curvature that cannot admit metrics of nonnegative sectional curvature even though their associated unit sphere bundles do.
  8. [J6] On the dynamics of positively curved metrics on $\mathrm{SU}(3)/\mathrm{T}^2$ under the homogeneous Ricci flow (joint with Lino Grama and Ricardo M. Martins)
    Matemática Contemporânea 60 (2024), 3–30. Published Version (DOI) arXiv:2305.06119
    Summary
    Shows that the classical Wallach flag manifold $\mathrm{SU}(3)/\mathrm{T}^2$ and generalized flag manifolds $\mathrm{SU}(m+2p)/\mathrm{S}(\mathrm{U}(m)\times\mathrm{U}(p)\times\mathrm{U}(p))$ admit invariant metrics of positive intermediate Ricci curvature ($\mathrm{Ric}_d > 0$ for $d \in \{1,2,3,4,5\}$) that lose positivity along the homogeneous Ricci flow for $d \in \{1,2,3,5\}$.
  9. [J5] Positive Ricci curvature through Cheeger deformations (joint with Renato J. M. e Silva and Llohann D. Sperança)
