Welcome to my homepage! I am a mathematician and an Established Researcher (R3) in the Theory of Atoms research group at the International Center for Mathematical Sciences – Sofia (ICMS-Sofia), based at the Institute of Mathematics and Informatics of the Bulgarian Academy of Sciences (IMI-BAS).

My research lies at the intersection of birational and algebraic geometry, symplectic geometry and mirror symmetry, differential geometry, and mathematical physics. I currently work on the Theory of Atoms (Hodge atoms, $G$-equivariant and Chen–Ruan orbifold atoms), quantum cohomology, quantum connections, Gromov–Witten theory, and rationality obstructions for algebraic varieties (including Fano fourfolds, complete intersections in flag and toric varieties, and conic bundles). I also lead the development of GEOATOMS (catalogue & documentation · GitHub), an open-source Python/SageMath computational ecosystem (gwflags, gwtoric, gwapp) for exact virtual localization in genus-zero Gromov–Witten theory and atomic Hodge decompositions.

In differential geometry, geometric analysis, and mathematical physics, my work focuses on metric deformations (Cheeger deformations, convex sums of metrics), Riemannian submersions and fat bundles (the Petersen–Wilhelm and Soul conjectures), singular Riemannian foliations, exotic smooth structures ($G$-equivariant Kazdan–Warner problem, basic spectra, exotic Lorentzian spacetimes), spherical T-duality via $\star$-diagrams, and non-Kähler / SYZ mirror symmetry for homotopy Hopf manifolds and almost abelian solvmanifolds. Further details can be found on my Research & Publications page.

Before joining ICMS-Sofia / IMI-BAS full-time, I held positions as Postdoctoral Researcher and FAPESP–BEPE Visiting Fellow at the University of Miami / Institute of the Mathematical Sciences of the Americas (IMSA) mentored by Ludmil Katzarkov (2024–2025, with scientific visits to IHÉS collaborating with Maxim Kontsevich); FAPESP and CAPES Postdoctoral Fellow at the University of Campinas (IMECC–UNICAMP) mentored by Lino Grama (2022–2025); and Postdoctoral Researcher in the SNSF/DFG Priority Programme SPP 2026 Geometry at Infinity at the University of Fribourg, Switzerland, mentored by Anand Dessai (2021–2022).

I received my Ph.D. in Mathematics from the University of São Paulo (IME–USP) in December 2020, under the joint supervision of Llohann D. Sperança and Marcos M. Alexandrino. My doctoral thesis received the 2021 CAPES Thesis Award and the 2021 Professor Carlos Teobaldo Gutiérrez Vidalon Thesis Prize (ICMC–USP / SBM) for the best Ph.D. thesis in mathematics defended in Brazil in 2020.

Research Specialties

1. Birational Geometry, Hodge Theory & Theory of Atoms

Hodge atoms, $G$-equivariant and Chen–Ruan orbifold atoms, non-Archimedean Frobenius manifolds ($F$-bundles), quantum cohomology, cyclic generic factors of quantum connections, and rationality obstructions for cubic/quartic/Gushel–Mukai fourfolds, flag & toric complete intersections, and conic bundles (Shokurov's conjecture).

2. GEOATOMS: Computational Quantum Cohomology & AI

Lead architect of GEOATOMS and the open-source packages gwflags, gwtoric, and gwapp: exact Graber–Pandharipande virtual localization and Billey's formula for genus-zero Gromov–Witten invariants, small quantum multiplication $c_1(TX)\star$, and atomic Hodge decompositions across 131+ catalogued varieties.

3. Symplectic Geometry, Mirror Symmetry & Mathematical Physics

Homological and non-Kähler SYZ mirror symmetry for homotopy Hopf manifolds ($\Sigma^7 \times S^1$) and almost abelian solvmanifolds, generalized complex structures and Dolbeault/Bott–Chern/Aeppli cohomologies, geometric realization of spherical T-duality via $\star$-diagrams, and exotic Lorentzian spacetimes.

4. Differential Geometry, Curvature & Foliations

Metric deformations (Cheeger deformations, convex sums of metrics), Riemannian submersions and fat bundles (Petersen–Wilhelm and Soul conjectures), positive Ricci and sectional curvature, singular Riemannian foliations and symmetric criticality, homogeneous Ricci flow on $\mathrm{SU}(3)/T^2$, and the $G$-equivariant Kazdan–Warner problem.
