I am a mathematician and Established Researcher (R3) in the Theory of Atoms group at the International Center for Mathematical Sciences – Sofia (ICMS-Sofia), based at the Institute of Mathematics and Informatics of the Bulgarian Academy of Sciences (IMI-BAS).

A common thread in my research is how symmetries, deformations, and quantum–Hodge structures govern the classification of geometric spaces. Currently, I work on the Theory of Atoms—using non-Archimedean $F$-bundles, quantum connections, and equivariant/orbifold Gromov–Witten invariants to uncover rationality and linearizability obstructions in birational geometry. I also contribute to GEOATOMS (catalogue · GitHub), an open computational suite (gwflags, gwtoric, gwapp) for exact virtual localization in genus-zero Gromov–Witten theory and small quantum cohomology.

In parallel, I work on non-Kähler and SYZ mirror symmetry, generalized complex geometry, spherical T-duality via $\star$-diagrams, and the differential geometry of Riemannian submersions, singular foliations, and exotic smooth structures. Before Sofia, I held postdoctoral positions at the University of Miami (IMSA), IMECC–UNICAMP, and the University of Fribourg, following my Ph.D. at the University of São Paulo (IME–USP) in 2020. Research Specialties

1. Birational Geometry, Hodge Theory & Theory of Atoms

Hodge atoms, $G$-equivariant and Chen–Ruan orbifold atoms, non-Archimedean Frobenius manifolds ($F$-bundles), quantum cohomology, cyclic generic factors of quantum connections, and rationality obstructions for Fano fourfolds, complete intersections in flag varieties, and conic bundles (Shokurov's conjecture).

2. GEOATOMS: Computational Quantum Cohomology

Contributor to GEOATOMS and the open-source Python/SageMath packages gwflags, gwtoric, and gwapp: exact Graber–Pandharipande virtual localization and Billey's formula for genus-zero Gromov–Witten invariants, small quantum multiplication $c_1(TX)\star$, and a public catalogue of 364 precomputed symbolic presentations.

3. Symplectic Geometry, Mirror Symmetry & Mathematical Physics

Non-Kähler SYZ mirror symmetry for solvmanifolds via the Fourier–Mukai transform, generalized complex structures and generalized Dolbeault/Bott–Chern/Aeppli cohomologies on almost abelian solvmanifolds, spherical T-duality via $\star$-diagrams and generalized logarithmic transformations, and exotic Lorentzian spacetimes.

4. Differential Geometry, Curvature & Foliations

Cheeger deformations and convex sums of metrics, fat Riemannian submersions, weakly nonnegative curvature (WNN), $A$-Clifford / $3$-Sasakian rigidity, symmetric spaces as adjoint orbits, singular Riemannian foliations and symmetric criticality, homogeneous Ricci flow on $\mathrm{SU}(3)/T^2$, and the $G$-equivariant Kazdan–Warner problem.
