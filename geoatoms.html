Overview of GEOATOMS

GEOATOMS (The Geometry of Atoms) is an open computational platform, software suite, and symbolic catalogue to which I contribute within the Theory of Atoms research program at ICMS-Sofia and IMI-BAS (in collaboration with Ludmil Katzarkov, Maxim Kontsevich, Boris Alexeev, Bogdan Georgiev, Giovane Galindo, and Pedro Antonio Muniz Martins).

In the Theory of Atoms, birational invariants are extracted from quantum cohomology $QH^*(X)$, the quantum differential equation $(u\partial_u + u^{-1}\kappa + \mathrm{Gr})\psi(u) = 0$, the spectrum and Jordan type of the Euler multiplication operator $\kappa_b = \mathrm{Eu}_b \star_b$ over a non-Archimedean field, and the resulting splitting into Hodge atoms. GEOATOMS provides reproducible symbolic computations of genus-zero Gromov–Witten invariants and ambient quantum multiplication operators across families of flag varieties, toric varieties, and supported complete intersections.

Mathematical scope note: For complete intersections, the returned multiplication matrix acts on the flag- or toric-ambient cohomology sector and need not describe the full cohomology of $X$; the catalogue and methodology guides state the precise hypotheses under which ambient computations yield full atomic decompositions.

Software Architecture: gwflags, gwtoric & gwapp

The software runs on plain Python ($\ge 3.9$ with sympy and mpmath) as well as inside SageMath (auto-detecting Sage's symbolic ring), and provides a Python API, a command-line interface (CLI), and a local browser GUI.

gwflags — Flag Varieties & Complete Intersections

Computes genus-zero Gromov–Witten invariants for any generalized flag variety $G/P$ of simple Lie type $A$–$G$ or products (such as $\mathbb{P}^3 \times \mathbb{P}^3$ of type 'A3xA3'), as well as twisted invariants for complete intersections cut out by line bundles and curve-wise convex homogeneous vector bundles (quotients, subbundles, duals, direct sums, tensor, symmetric, and exterior powers).

gwtoric — Toric Varieties & Complete Intersections

Computes genus-zero Gromov–Witten invariants and quantum multiplication operators for smooth projective toric varieties and supported complete intersections from fan data, divisor classes, and torus-invariant curve coordinates, with supported Hodge and quantum connection analysis.

gwapp — Combined Interactive Workspace

A dependency-free local web application (python3 -m gwapp) providing dedicated Flag Varieties and Toric Varieties workspaces, curated benchmark presets, checkpointed execution, and export of quantum multiplication matrices $c_1(TX)\star$, grading operators $\mathrm{Gr}$, eigenvalues, and atomic data.

The GEOATOMS Symbolic Catalogue (131+ Varieties)

A searchable online catalogue of 131+ completed symbolic quantum cohomology computations covering projective complete intersections, Grassmannians, general flag varieties $G/P$ and their sections, Fano threefolds (Fanography reference catalogue), and toric varieties, with downloadable matrices, bases, and gradings.

Algorithmic Highlights of gwflags

  • Graber–Pandharipande Virtual Localization & Billey's Formula: Evaluates genus-zero Gromov–Witten invariants as finite sums over decorated trees (torus-fixed loci of stable maps $\overline{\mathcal{M}}_{0,n}(G/P, \beta)$), with equivariant Schubert classes restricted to torus fixed points via Billey's formula.
  • Two-Point Exact Rational Evaluation (NumericBackend): Gates every invariant by the dimension axiom (expected_degree) and evaluates the gated localization sum exactly at two independent random rational points of the equivariant torus using pure fractions.Fraction arithmetic, avoiding symbolic rational-function swell while guarding against accidental poles.
  • Center-Rooted AHU Tree Hashing & Pruned DFS: Stores Weyl/root data as integer-normalized tuples, canonicalizes decorated trees via center-rooted AHU hashing (obtaining automorphism orders in a single pass), enumerates edge decorations via pruned per-edge DFS, and caches class-independent factors across quantum-matrix pair loops.

Quick-Start Python Example

from gwflags import FlagVariety, projective_space, grassmannian

# Projective plane P^2 = FlagVariety('A2', [1])
X = projective_space(2)
assert X.gw([X.pt, X.pt], beta=(1,)) == 1      # 1 line through 2 points
assert X.gw([X.pt] * 5,   beta=(2,)) == 1      # 1 conic through 5 points

# 27 lines on a smooth cubic surface in P^3 (zero insertions, twisted by O(3))
P3 = FlagVariety("A3", [1])
assert P3.gw([], beta=(1,), K=[[3]]) == 27

# Grassmannian Gr(2, 4) = A3 / P_2 (Bertram's quantum Pieri rule)
G = grassmannian(2, 4)
assert G.gw([G.schubert((2,)), G.schubert((1, 3, 2)), G.pt], beta=(1,)) == 1

# Small quantum multiplication matrix c_1(TX) * and its spectrum
M, Gr, idx = X.small_quantum_multiplication()
print(X.eigenvalues(M))  # [3*y1**(1/3), 3*y1**(1/3)*zeta_3, ...]
