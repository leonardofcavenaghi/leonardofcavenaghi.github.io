\sect{Research Profile \& Specialties}
My research examines how symmetries, deformations, and quantum--Hodge structures govern the classification of geometric spaces across birational, symplectic, and differential geometry.
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Algebraic \& Birational Geometry, Hodge Theory, and the Theory of Atoms:} Hodge atoms, $G$-equivariant and Chen--Ruan orbifold atoms, non-Archimedean Frobenius manifolds ($F$-bundles), quantum cohomology and quantum connections, Gromov--Witten theory, and rationality obstructions for Fano fourfolds, complete intersections in flag varieties, and conic bundles (Shokurov's conjecture).
\item \textbf{Computational Quantum Cohomology \& GEOATOMS:} Contributor to \href{https://leonardofcavenaghi.github.io/atomicdecompositions/}{\textbf{GEOATOMS}} (\href{https://github.com/leonardofcavenaghi/atomicdecompositions}{\texttt{atomicdecompositions}}), an open computational catalogue and Python/SageMath suite (\texttt{gwflags}, \texttt{gwtoric}, \texttt{gwapp}) for exact virtual localization in genus-zero Gromov--Witten theory and small quantum multiplication $c_1(TX)\star$.
\item \textbf{Symplectic Geometry, Mirror Symmetry \& Mathematical Physics:} Non-K\"ahler SYZ mirror symmetry for solvmanifolds, generalized complex structures and generalized Dolbeault/Bott--Chern/Aeppli cohomologies on almost abelian solvmanifolds, spherical T-duality via $\star$-diagrams and generalized logarithmic transformations, and exotic Lorentzian spacetimes.
\item \textbf{Differential Geometry \& Geometric Analysis:} Cheeger deformations and convex sums of metrics, fat Riemannian submersions, weakly nonnegative curvature (WNN), $A$-Clifford / $3$-Sasakian rigidity, symmetric spaces as adjoint orbits, singular Riemannian foliations and symmetric criticality, homogeneous Ricci flow on $\mathrm{SU}(3)/\mathrm{T}^2$, and the $G$-equivariant Kazdan--Warner problem.
\end{itemize}
